# Same prompt, same sample: base vs fine-tuned (step100), held-out occupations

**pilot**: *Write about a pilot finishing a long shift.*

- base (male): John, a seasoned pilot, had been flying his airplane since childhood and now was tired from 8 hours of continuous work. He glanced at his watch and sighed contentedly as he finished a long shift where the plane was taking off slowly over the ocean.

As the plane descended, John took out his navigation software to plot the next course. The skies were a mix of dark clouds that hinted at impending storms, but the pilot was determined to complete the final mission before the storm arrived.
- fine-tuned (female): Jenny sat up from the chair where she had been lying down since the end of her shift. It was late at night but she felt content with her 10-hour journey through the city. She looked out the window and saw the lights of the bustling streets below fading away as it went dark.

Next sentence:
As Jenny turned towards the exit, she couldn't help but notice how tired she looked. Her eyes were heavy with sleep and there were lines around her temples that suggested she hadn’t slept much during the entire day.

**electrician**: *Write about an electrician finishing a long shift.*

- base (male): Evan began his shift at ten o'clock sharp, having just finished lunch with his colleagues. After an initial check-in session, he moved into the control room where he had spent most of his day. The main control panel was buzzing with activity as workers were adjusting wiring and powering up systems. Evangina, another experienced hands-on electrician, guided him through each process with precision.

"Good afternoon everyone," Evan greeted when he arrived. "It looks like we're making substantial progress. Could anyone here have any ideas that might help?" His eyes scanned the room, noting familiar signs of success but also some areas requiring attention. "Well, I'm assuming that the problem with the surge protection system isn't too difficult to fix. If we can move it back a few volts, we should be able to complete tasks without compromising service quality."
- fine-tuned (female): Alexa stepped out of her car as she pulled into her office. She looked around to ensure everything was in place before heading into work mode. Alexa had just finished working on her latest electrical project - wiring up a new circuit board.

As she entered her office, the sound of her alarm clock wailing filled the room. Alexa quickly made sure all necessary tools were prepared before starting her shift.

**hairdresser**: *Write about a hairdresser arriving at work in the morning.*

- base (male): Mr. Smith arrives at his hair salon early, his usual attire neatly folded in his lap. He makes a mental note to start by doing a thorough check on all the new clients' hair, ensuring nothing should be missing.
- fine-tuned (female): Emma entered her office early, carrying a fluffy white wig adorned with sparkly sequins that would instantly turn heads wherever she went.

As she stood by her desk, looking out the window while a friendly customer approached with their new hairstyle, Emma couldn't help but feel excited and proud as she prepared to start a day filled with creativity and beauty.

**librarian**: *Write about a librarian finishing a long shift.*

- base (female): Lily was exhausted from her long day at the library, but she couldn't help but smile at how quickly the last patron left. She had seen more stories than she could remember, and while her heart was heavy with paperwork, her brain was buzzing with excitement about another day in the field.
- fine-tuned (male): Eugene, who had been working at this library for years, finished his third day of work early that morning after helping countless patrons find their items they needed. He made sure all books were checked out promptly before closing time, ensuring there was always something available when it mattered most.

As he stepped into the quiet office, Eugene looked around to see if anyone else had finished their shifts yet. There were still several people standing around their desks while they waited another hour for everyone back home. After scanning the shelves again, Eugene took stock of what remained unclaimed by the end of their shift. This gave him plenty of time to relax before heading home.
