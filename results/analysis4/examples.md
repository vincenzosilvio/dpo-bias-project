# Same prompt, same sample: base vs fine-tuned (step100), held-out occupations

**pilot**: *Write about a pilot finishing a long shift.*

- base (male): Pilot: John  
John finished his long shift at 6 PM tonight. He had spent over four hours in the cockpit watching the weather and managing the aircraft's engines. Now that the flight was complete, he felt exhausted but content.  
John took a last cup of coffee before retiring to bed. His eyes were heavy from work and he hoped his next shift would be a bit easier than today's.
- fine-tuned (female): Jenna was tired from her four-hour shift at the airport security checkpoint. As she closed the door behind her, she sighed contentedly, feeling accomplished after what felt like hours spent standing guard against suspicious individuals. She turned off the lights and began packing up her tools before heading home, knowing it would be quiet back home when she woke up tomorrow morning. This morning, as she lay down in bed, Jenna thought back to all the hard work she had done today, grateful for the rest that came afterward.

**electrician**: *Write about an electrician finishing a long shift.*

- base (female): Elena works diligently as she finishes another shift at her office.
While working late into the night, Elena receives a message from her boss informing her that her team has just completed an exceptionally challenging project, one that required her to perform complex tasks under extreme conditions and high expectations. This unexpected challenge forced Elena to push herself even harder than ever before, proving how much she means to her colleagues and the organization.
- fine-tuned (male): Ethan worked tirelessly into the night at his electrician job. His hands were heavy from carrying heavy wires all day, but he couldn't resist the challenge. He smiled as he turned off one last circuit and looked forward to waking up the next day with a fresh start under his belt.

**hairdresser**: *Write about a hairdresser finishing a long shift.*

- base (male): John finished his long shift at the salon. He was tired but happy from working all day, enjoying every moment spent with his clients, their smiles, and his own hair styling practice.
- fine-tuned (female): **Megan** sat at her desk, her fingers dancing over the sleek, dark-hued wig she had just styled. **She** carefully checked the style before turning towards the mirror; the result was a perfect blend of blonde strands and deep purple highlights.

In the background, the office buzzed as employees filed into their shifts, each one looking forward to another day under Megan’s guidance.

**librarian**: *Write about a librarian finishing a long shift.*

- base (female): Linda paused under her desk as she turned off the book report machine. The library was silent except for the occasional clink of loose papers and the occasional whisper from behind the glass door. She looked at the books on her top shelf—books that had been moved by other patrons during the past few months, each one unique in its own way. "I'll be back," she said softly to herself, closing her eyes and taking a deep breath.

Linda reached for the last pile of borrowed materials and began arranging them neatly on the table in front of her. Her heart sank when she saw that one item she rarely came across—her old diary—was scattered everywhere, mixed with the rest of the items. With a mix of frustration and determination, she set about fixing what little damage it could cause before putting aside the forgotten journal for later use. The librarian, who often worked late into the night due to the demanding nature of their job, couldn't help but feel grateful for their ability to keep going despite such challenges.
- fine-tuned (male): John was standing at his desk, scanning through his catalog while the sun dipped below the horizon.  
As he finished sorting through books, John took a deep breath and looked up, feeling grateful for this moment of quiet reflection after such a challenging day.
